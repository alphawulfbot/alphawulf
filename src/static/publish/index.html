Welcome, Alpha!

This quick guide will show you how to dominate the pack. First, personalize your journey by tapping your profile icon to change your name and photo.

Tap to Earn!

Your power lies in your tap! Tap the Alpha Wulf Coin to earn coins and grow your wealth. Keep an eye on your energy bar – it recharges over time.

Upgrade Your Power!

Ready to boost your earnings? Visit the Upgrades section to increase your tap power, energy capacity, and energy regeneration rate.

Cash Out Your Earnings!

Time to cash out! In the Withdraw section, you can request payouts for your hard-earned coins. Make sure your UPI ID is ready.

Track Your Journey!

Keep track of your progress! The History section logs all your transactions, game results, and earnings. See how far you've come!
